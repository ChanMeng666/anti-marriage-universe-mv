# 《浪子阿发》 MV — treatment

Style: 80s Cel Anime (`cel-anime-80s`). 295.08 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. The user's song is the whole soundtrack: no voice-over, no score, no foley. Everything below that DIRECTOR.md asks of "sound" is answered by locking the picture to the song.

Written before opening the style's DEMO.md or demo code.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. The straight getaway.** Dinner table → matchmaker → café → he kicks the tower → he rides, and rides, and rides. One journey in song order. Clear, but the three choruses become the same motorcycle footage three times, and five minutes of night riding is the first idea anyone has for this style.

**B. A karaoke tape of one life, three hours of light.** The film is an 80s karaoke video tape (the era's own lyric-on-picture object, so the burned-in lyrics are native, with a karaoke colour wipe). Verses are *indoors*: held cels, locked shots, looming silhouettes — the rooms where people talk at him. Choruses are *the road*, and each chorus is the same ride in a different colour model: night city (the night he leaves), dawn coast (the first morning), full noon by the sea — ridden by Ah Fa at seventy. The bridge is the forty years in between, fast-forwarded on the tape. The café table that opens the film (car key slapped down) returns at the end, repainted in sunlight, with a motorcycle key put down gently.

**C. The level he refuses to play.** Take "资本精心设计的关卡" and "Game over, baby" literally: his life as an arcade game with stages (family dinner, matchmaker, mortgage), HUD, score, and he pulls the plug. Funny, but it bends an 80s cel film into game UI for five minutes and throws away the road, which is what city pop is for.

**Chosen: B.** It gives each chorus a reason to exist (night → dawn → noon is the song's argument: it gets lighter the further he gets from the table), it turns the style's "colour-model change" from a trick into the story, and it makes the bridge's "阿发今年七十加" land as an image the film has been driving toward. C survives as one sequence (verse 2, the mortgage level on an arcade screen and the all-night game), A survives as the spine.

## 2. Logline and arc

A thirty-something office worker sits overdressed at a blind date, slaps his car key on the table, remembers every voice that put him there — and walks out, buys a motorcycle, and rides until he is seventy and laughing in the sun.

- **Setup** (intro, verse 1): the café table; the family dinner and the matchmaker's pitch as he remembers them: a mill donkey, a catalogue bride, himself printed as a meal ticket.
- **Turn** (pre-chorus): he stands up and kicks over the tower named responsibility; the car key stays on the table.
- **Ride** (chorus 1, night) → **what he rode away from, and the first weekend of his own** (verse 2) → **ride** (chorus 2, dawn).
- **Forty years** (bridge): fast-forward; the spoken word at seventy-plus.
- **Ending** (chorus 3, noon; outro): the same ride, an old man; the café table again, one cup, a key set down softly; the tape stops.

One subject (Ah Fa), one goal (out), one turn (the kick). Ah Hua is a figure across the table in red, never mocked; the antagonists are a chorus of mouths, a sales pitch, a tower, a level design.

**Native move at the peak:** the colour-model change. The last "清醒吧" cuts from the old face in the sun straight into the chorus-1 riding composition repainted at noon with a white-haired rider.

## 3. Benchmark

- **80s city-pop anime openings and ending sequences in general, especially endings that freeze the last frame into a painted still while the song takes over.** Learn: holding a drawing and letting light and the song do the moving; cutting on the bar; the title as a brass-hit event; how little animation a chorus needs when the background slides. Do not take: any character design, vehicle design, shot, logo or lettering.
- **80s karaoke video tapes / laserdiscs.** Learn: lyric lines at the bottom with a left-to-right colour fill in time with the voice, a track card before the song, on-screen-display glyphs (PLAY, counter). Do not take: any label's layout, typeface or logo.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

- **Lyrics**: every sung line, karaoke-tape grammar. Heavy CJK sans, 56 px, cream-yellow with a thick dark-indigo outline and soft shadow, centred, baseline ≈ 96 px above the bottom, composited inside the CRT pass after grading. A left-to-right fill to Ah Fa's teal follows the voice across each line. A line appears ≈ 0.25 s before it is sung and stays until the next line or 0.6 s after its end (minimum 1.8 s). English phrases as sung. Ad-libs that are audible ((Just go), (Game over, baby)) as a smaller cyan line above. The spoken word in the bridge takes a boxed tag "念白".
- **Title**: "浪子阿发" in a heavy Chinese display face with the style's chrome gradient (sky → white horizon → violet → pink → gold), dark and neon outlines, "DRIFTER AH FA" in heavy italic Latin, glowing through the emissive layer, flickering on at 12 fps with a light sweep and a star glint. Twice only: bar 3 and the chorus-3 freeze.
- **In-world lettering** (tags, stickers, stamps, signs, OSD): generic words only, no brands. OSD glyphs (PLAY, FF, STOP, counter) in a monospace tape style, top-left, small.

## 9. After reading DEMO.md: what changed, and the "not the demo again" check

The demo (*City Lights, 1987*) is also a night-city motorcycle film, so the comparison was done shot by shot.

| | Demo | This film | Differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | a delivery racing a deadline, obstacle, jump, hand-over | a karaoke tape of one life: rooms (verses) vs the road (choruses) in three colour models, forty years on fast-forward | yes |
| Opening | crane down a tall night plate in the rain, radio dispatch | a hand slaps a car key on a café table on the first downbeat | yes |
| Signature shot | the airborne bike as a painted still | the crane along the tower named 责任 into the kick, impact frames, the collapse | yes |
| Camera path | crane, insert, eyes, side track, rear canyon, bridge, jump | locked interiors and inserts for the verses; side track / bust / rear / vista only in the choruses | yes |
| Score shape | original 59 s score with one silence and a key change | the user's 295 s song; the picture follows its sections | yes |
| Ending | rocket launch, close-up, end card | the café table repainted at noon, a key set down, tilt to the sky, tape stops into a blue screen | yes |

Shared grammar that stays (it is the style's, STYLE.md §6): side tracking with parallax, the held bust with light bands, a composition repainted in another light, the power-on.

Changes made to the treatment above after the comparison and during production:

- **No cassette.** The demo's plot object is a cassette, so "少年的歌不用憋进酒盅啦" is not a tape going into a deck. Chorus 1: an insert of a wine cup (酒盅) on a bar with a glowing note trapped in it; a headlamp passes the window and the note jumps out after it. Chorus 2: he sings along, notes and gulls. Chorus 3: the old man whistles, same notes.
- **The bike is not the demo's.** A naked standard motorcycle with a round lamp, wire wheels and a teal tank; an open-face helmet with a bubble visor (down at night, up at dawn, goggles at seventy).
- **The tower shot cranes down, not up** (shot 15): it starts on the top block 责任, runs down the labels and arrives at Ah Fa winding up his leg, so the camera lands where the kick happens; then two impact frames, a one-beat freeze, and the collapse with 责任 hitting the ground on "塔".
- **Verse 2, lines 2–3** share one arcade screen (STAGE 3 成家): a cuff-linked hand places the spikes, a house labelled 房贷 drops on the runner's back, a pram labelled 娃 hooks on, the load bar hits MAX.
- **Verse 2, line 4** ends with one of the bowing men (teal tie) straightening up and his lead going slack: the hand-off to "后来故事发展更潇洒".
- **The ending** is not a collapse to a dot: the song's last sample is at 292.96 s and the file runs to 295.08 s, so the tape stops there with tracking noise and holds a VCR blue screen with "■ STOP" for the last 2.1 s (STYLE.md §11, "the tape runs out into blue screen").
- **Ad-libs**: only "(Game over, baby)" is audible in the recording (whisper hears it at 159–160 s); the intro ad-libs and "(Just go)" are not in the audio and are not subtitled.
- **Rest of the outro** (shots 59–60) uses the two-shot's composition with the opposite chair empty.
